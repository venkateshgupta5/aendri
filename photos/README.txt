💌 PHOTOS FOLDER
================

Two ways to add your wife's photos:

OPTION 1 — In the app (easiest, recommended)
  Open index.html, tap the ⚙️ Setup button on the welcome screen,
  and choose photos from your device. They are saved privately on
  that device (nothing is uploaded anywhere). You can also edit each
  caption there.

OPTION 2 — This folder
  Drop image files here and name them in order:
      1.jpg   2.jpg   3.jpg   4.jpg   5.jpg
  (.jpg, .jpeg, .png, and .webp all work)
  They will load automatically, in order, into the gallery and the
  welcome / final screens.

Notes
  - Photos added in the app always override files in this folder.
  - 5 photos is the sweet spot, but it works beautifully with 1 or 2.
  - Portrait or landscape both work; images are never stretched.
